The Pragati Story

A Journey of Engineering Precision & Trust

Pragati Engineers is a trusted manufacturer of Precision Industrial Rollers, serving diverse manufacturing and process industries with a resolute focus on quality, accuracy, reliability, and engineering excellence.

Our journey began with Mr. Jayantibhai Panchal, who has been associated with the roller industry since 1985. With decades of hands-on experience, deep technical understanding of roller dynamics, precision fabrication, balancing, and demanding industrial requirements, this rich technical foundation became the bedrock of Pragati Engineers.

In 2010, Pragati Engineers was formally established with a clear vision: to engineer and provide world-class, high-precision rollers to industries across India and beyond.

Today, we specialize in manufacturing Precision Industrial Rollers and Weaving Loom Cloth Rollers engineered to perform in demanding, high-speed, non-stop industrial cycles. Our modern manufacturing capabilities, two dedicated manufacturing facilities, and skilled engineering team enable us to produce rollers with exceptionally high dimensional accuracy, micro-fine surface finish, dynamic balancing, and rock-solid dependability.

Manufacturing Discipline

The 7 Pillars of Pragati Engineering

Every roller manufactured in our plants passes through these rigorous technical benchmarks.

1. Material Selection

Premium grade seamless carbon steel pipes, forged alloy steel shafts (EN8/EN9/EN19), and high-strength 6000-series aluminium alloys with ultrasonic flaw certification.

2. Dimensional Accuracy

Precision CNC turning and cylindrical grinding maintaining micrometric tolerances on bearing journals, shaft steps, and face diameters.

3. Concentricity

True centerline alignment across the entire length of the roller barrel and shaft extensions to eliminate wobbling and eccentric motion.

4. Runout Control

Dial gauge radial and axial Total Indicator Reading (TIR) verified under < 0.01 mm to 0.02 mm, preventing web flutter and uneven cloth winding.

5. Surface Finish

Mirror-finish hard chrome, diamond knurling, spiral groove routing, or elastomeric rubber coatings according to application requirements.

6. Dynamic Balancing

Precision electronic two-plane balancing in accordance with ISO 1940 standard (G2.5 / G1.6 grades) preventing vibration at operating speeds.

7. Overall Dependability

Comprehensive mechanical stress relief, Lorch welding integrity, and Robologic robotic automation ensuring extended lifespan.
