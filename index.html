Rigorous Quality Assurance

Every Roller Is Engineered For Zero-Defect Performance

Every roller at Pragati Engineers undergoes strict quality validation across 5 critical parameters before dispatch to safeguard our customer's machinery.

01

Material Selection

Strict ultrasonic testing and chemical analysis of seamless pipes, aluminium alloys, and EN-grade steel bars.

02

Concentricity & Runout

Total Indicator Reading (TIR) verified on precision dial gauges under < 0.01 mm for runout-free rotation.

03

Dynamic Balancing

Dual-plane electronic balancing eliminating centrifugal force, vibrations, and premature bearing wear.

04

Surface Finish (Ra)

Surface roughness verified with digital profilometers ensuring scratch-free, mirror or knurled textures.

05

Dimension Inspection

Micrometer and vernier inspection of bearing journals, keyways, and circlip grooves to ISO tolerance standards.
