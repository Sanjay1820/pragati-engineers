Focused Facilities for Uncompromised Quality

Pragati Engineers operates through two dedicated manufacturing units, each specialized in its respective product segment. This focused infrastructure enables us to maintain better process control, efficient production planning, and consistent quality across our complete range of rollers.

By decoupling our Loom Cloth Roller production line from our Custom Industrial Precision Roller engineering cell, our engineering teams maintain strict cycle times, specialized tooling, and zero cross-contamination in balancing and surface finishing operations.

"Complete in-house capability from raw material saw cutting, CNC turning, key slotting, and robotic welding to dynamic balancing and hard chrome plating."
Dual Specialization

Our Dedicated Manufacturing Units

Strategically located in Swarnim Industrial Park, Bakrol Bujrang Road, Bakrol, Ahmedabad.

Unit 1 – Specialized Facility

Loom Cloth Roller Manufacturing

Our dedicated Loom Cloth Roller manufacturing unit is equipped for the complete manufacturing and finishing of Cloth Rollers for high-speed weaving looms.

The facility supports manufacturing of Mild Steel (MS) and Aluminium Cloth Rollers for Japanese, European, and Chinese weaving looms, covering loom widths from 170 cm to 400 cm.

Key Capabilities:

  • High precision extrusion & tube turning up to 400 cm length
  • Tsudakoma, Toyota, Picanol, Itema, Sulzer & Somet configurations
  • Dynamic balancing rig calibrated for high RPM loom speeds
  • Interference fit thermal hot assembly of shafts
A-6, Swarnim Industrial Park Bakrol Bujrang Road, Ahmedabad - 382430
Unit 2 – Specialized Facility

Industrial Precision Roller Manufacturing

Our second dedicated facility is focused on the manufacturing of Industrial Precision Rollers for various industrial applications.

The unit is designed to handle precision machining, fabrication, assembly, surface finishing, balancing, and inspection requirements for rollers manufactured according to customer-specific dimensions and technical specifications.

Key Capabilities:

  • Mild Steel, Stainless Steel (304/316) & Coated Rollers
  • Double-shell cooling/heating internal spiral channels
  • Mirror hard chrome plating and cylindrical grinding
  • Custom engineering according to OEM drawing specifications
A-119, Swarnim Industrial Park Bakrol Bujrang Road, Ahmedabad - 382430
Advanced Technology

Our Complete In-House Machinery Setup

Full ownership of machinery ensures total process control, micro-tolerance concentricity, and on-time dispatches.

Heavy Precision

CNC Lathe Machine

Heavy-duty computer numerical control lathes capable of long-bed turning of roller barrels, bearing journal steps, and threading with repeatability under 5 microns.

Stock Preparation

CNC Bandsaw Machine

High-speed automatic band sawing of thick-walled seamless pipes, alloy steel bars, and aluminium billets ensuring 90-degree square cuts and minimal material waste.

3-Axis Machining

VMC Machine

Vertical Machining Centers utilized for intricate drilling, pitch circle diameter (PCD) bolt holes on flange heads, internal boring, and precision end plate milling.

Zero Vibration

Dynamic Balancing Machine

High-precision electronic two-plane dynamic balancing test station to remove unbalance and verify rollers run smoothly up to high operating RPM without machinery resonance.

Thermal Shrink-Fit

Roller Hot Assembly

Induction heating and interference shrink-fit assembly setup to fuse roller shafts and end flanges into the pipe barrel for permanent, vibration-proof torque transfer.

Transmission Drive

CNC Key Slot Machine

Automated keyway milling machine ensuring precise depth, width, and parallelism tolerances matching Japanese, European, and DIN standard motor & gear couplings.

German Engineering

Welding Machine (Lorch Make)

World-class German Lorch inverter welding systems providing deep, spatter-free weld penetration and uniform metallurgy across heavy roller end plugs.

Robotic Automation

Welding Automation (Robologic)

Automated circumferential robotic welding cells by Robologic India delivering 360-degree continuous automated weld seams with zero porosity and maximum tensile load support.

Surface Treatment

Hard Chrome Plating Plant

Specialized in-house electroplating facility providing uniform hard chrome deposition up to 65 HRC with high micro-crack density for corrosion immunity and wear resistance.

Micro-Finish Grinding

Cylindrical Grinding Machine

Precision cylindrical grinding for final OD sizing, achieving superfine surface finish (Ra values < 0.1 µm) and zero taper across lengthy roller faces.
