Product Engineering Overview

Pragati Engineers manufactures heavy-duty, high-precision cloth take-up rolls specifically designed for demanding high-speed weaving operations. Using premium seamless tubes and high-grade shafts, our rollers ensure uniform fabric tension, zero fabric slippage, and maximum production efficiency.

High concentricity across entire loom length (up to 4000 mm).
Precision surface finish (fluted, knurled, or coated) prevents fabric crease.
Heavy-duty end journals heat-treated for long bearing life.
100% interchangeable with Tsudakoma OEM specifications.
